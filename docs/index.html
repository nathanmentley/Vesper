Why Vesper

A music player,
not a music service.

Vesper is built around a simple idea: your music collection should remain yours. Your files, your server, your playlists, and your listening history should not require a subscription or another platform to make them useful.

It is deliberately a small desktop application, built with GTK 4 and libadwaita, with an architecture designed to keep music providers replaceable.

LOCAL

Your files

Play music from a collection stored on your own computer.

SERVER

Your library

Connect to a Subsonic-compatible music server such as Navidrome.

OPEN

Your software

Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later, and built with the Linux desktop in mind.
