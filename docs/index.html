A native music player for Linux

Music, your way.

Vesper is a fast, focused desktop music player for your own collection, with support for local libraries and Navidrome.

Built for your music

Your library. Your server. Your player.

Vesper is built around the music you already own and the servers you already run.

♫

Your library

Browse artists, albums, and songs from your local music collection.

☾

Navidrome

Connect to your Subsonic-compatible music server and bring your collection into Vesper.

▶

Queue first

Build a queue, reorder tracks, shuffle your music, and save queues as playlists.

⌘

Linux native

Built with GTK4 and libadwaita to feel at home on the Linux desktop.

♡

Your music

Vesper is designed around music libraries you control, rather than a catalog you rent.

⚙

Extensible

A provider-based architecture leaves room for local libraries, music servers, and future integrations.

Open source
